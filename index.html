Where Kavach stands today

This page states the current thinking, not a decision. Nothing here has been filtered, ranked, or finalized.

Kavach started as one idea: a wearable safety device for women that doesn't look like a safety device. Beyond that, almost everything is still open.

What's being explored so far, in no particular order:

  • A device disguised as ordinary jewelry — a ring, bracelet, necklace, or something else entirely.
  • A private code word that triggers something. What exactly it should do is still being worked out by the people answering the Join questions.
  • An app, legal groundwork, cybersecurity review, manufacturing, and testing — each running as its own project below.
  • Input from law, engineering, software, psychology, NGO work, and a dozen other fields, all treated as equally valid starting points.

What do you think? What would you change? What would you add?
